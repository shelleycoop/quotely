# Privacy policy for Quotely

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Quotely is a quotes reader. It is designed to work without an account and without sending your reading habits to a server.

## Data stored on the device

The app may store on your device:

- Quotes you mark as favorites
- Appearance preference (paper, ink, or system)
- Whether the daily reminder is enabled

This information is saved with on-device storage. It is not uploaded to us.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily quote reminder, Quotely uses the system notification permission to schedule **one local notification** around 8:00 each morning. These notifications are generated on the device. Quotely does not send remote push notifications.

## What we do not collect

Quotely does not collect:

- Names, emails, or account data
- Location
- Contacts
- Advertising identifiers for ads
- Analytics events
- Crash reports tied to an identity

The quote library is bundled in the app, so browsing works offline.

## Sharing

If you share a quote, the operating system share sheet opens. Any data you send through another app is governed by that app.

## Children

Quotely does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
